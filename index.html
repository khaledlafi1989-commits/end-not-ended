“I want a loaf.”

A three-year-old whispered it to me during the war in Gaza.

The story

I watched my mother bake half-loaves, counted with precision. In those months we learned to fear what we had never counted: water, bread, and a phone's battery percentage.

I survived the war. Now I'm fighting to make sure our stories survive too.

Out of all this, a desire to write was born in me: to take revenge on death by stealing its victims' stories from its mouth and immortalizing them on paper.

From it came my first novel, “An End That Has Not Yet Ended.” It is not a history book and does not try to explain the war. It captures what the news cannot: the fear, the waiting, the loss, and the human being who tries to remain human.

The novel is written and translated into English, but it needs your support to see the light.
